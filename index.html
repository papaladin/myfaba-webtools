// faba-encoder

Convert your MP3s to Faba Box format. No install, no upload, everything runs in your browser.
Based on myfaba-hacks. Original Faba (offline) only — not for Faba+.

01 — figure id

Use IDs between 2000–8999 to avoid conflicts with official Faba content.

02 — add your mp3 files

Drop MP3 files here

or click to browse — multiple files supported

    03 — install on your faba box

    1. Unzip the downloaded file

      You'll find a folder named K???? inside.

    2. Connect your Faba box to your computer

      Plug it in via USB while powered on. It should appear as a USB drive.

    3. Copy the folder to the Faba box

      Drag K???? into the root of the Faba drive. Don't rename it.

    4. Eject and unplug

      Safely eject the drive before unplugging.

    5. Write your NFC tag

      Open NFC Tools on your phone (iOS or Android). Tap Write → Add a record → Text and enter exactly:

      02190530????00

      Tap Write, then hold your NTAG213 tag to the back of your phone.

    6. Place tag on the Faba box

      Power on your Faba box and place the NFC tag on top. It should play your content.

    This tool only processes files you provide. No audio is uploaded anywhere — everything runs locally in your browser. Only use content you own or have rights to. This tool is not affiliated with MyFaba.
